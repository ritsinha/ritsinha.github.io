Somewhere around 2017 I stopped buying paper books. It was not a decision so much as a drift: a Kindle for travel, then the county library's Libby app, then audiobooks on Audible and, more recently, Spotify. The reading did not stop, but it scattered across four or five services, none of which knew about the others. I have often wanted a single list, and never had one.

So I built one. The list below is every book I have finished from 2017 to September 2026, as far as I can reconstruct it. I will admit to some cheating: a fair number of these were listened to rather than read, and I count them anyway. Le Carré read by Michael Jayston is, if anything, better than le Carré on the page.
